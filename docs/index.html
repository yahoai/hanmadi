Meeting transcripts for teams that switch languages mid-sentence

Drop in a recording. Hanmadi works out who spoke, in which language and what they said, and puts a translation under every line. It all runs on your Mac.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT). Apple Silicon Macs. Windows later.

What it does

Built for the meetings other transcribers get wrong

한

Language per line

Speech is cut where the speaker changes, and each piece gets its own language detection. A Japanese reply after a Korean question stays Japanese.

◉

Speakers with names

Tell Hanmadi who was there and which languages they speak. It matches each voice to a name instead of Speaker 1 and Speaker 2.

文

Translation underneath

Lines in other languages get a translation below the original. Switch between original, both, or translation only.

▶

Plays back with the text

Click a line to hear it. A map of who spoke when sits under the transcript, so you can jump to the part that mattered.

Aa

Remembers your words

Product names and people's names go into a glossary once. Transcription and translation spell them right from then on.

●

Voice Memos and recording

Import straight from iCloud Voice Memos, drop in any audio or video file, or record the meeting in the app.

How it works

Open models, run on your machine

Find speechSilero VAD skips the silence.
Tell voices apartpyannote and 3D-Speaker group the voices.
Cut at turnsShort pieces from the same person are joined again.
Write it downWhisper large-v3-turbo detects the language for each piece.
TranslateQwen3 4B translates line by line.

Runs on whisper.cpp, sherpa-onnx and llama.cpp. A 55-minute meeting takes about 25 minutes on an M1 Pro, in the background.

Privacy

Your meetings stay on your computer

Meeting audio is some of the most sensitive data a company has. Hanmadi has no server to send it to.

  • No account and no sign-in
  • Audio and transcripts are saved as plain files on your disk
  • Models are downloaded once, then everything works offline
  • The source is open, so you can check all of this yourself
Free and open source

Try it on your next meeting

The first launch downloads about 3 GB of models. After that, Hanmadi works without an internet connection.
